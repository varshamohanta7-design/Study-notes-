1. What are Essential Fatty Acids (EFAs)?

  • Essential Fatty Acids: Fatty acids that cannot be synthesized by the human body and MUST be supplied through the diet.
  • Out of various omega fatty acid families, Omega-3, Omega-6, and Omega-9 are the 3 most popular types in human nutrition.
  • Omega-3 & Omega-6: Families of Polyunsaturated Fats (PUFAs) that our body cannot produce on its own. Needs to be taken via food or dietary supplements.
  • Omega-9: Family of Monounsaturated Fats (MUFAs). The human body CAN synthesize Omega-9 from any unsaturated fats we consume!
    ➜ Note: Omega-9 is NOT strictly "essential", but replacing saturated fats with Omega-9 provides immense cardiovascular health benefits!

2. ω-3 (Omega-3) Fatty Acids

V. V. IMP! ⭐⭐⭐

📌 Definition: In ω-3 fatty acids, the first double bond appears at the 3rd carbon atom counting from the methyl (CH₃) end of the carbon chain.

EXAM TIP: Precursor Rule: α-Linolenic Acid (ALA) is the chemical precursor of EPA & DHA!

Major Members of ω-3:

  1. ALA: α-Linolenic acid C18:3 n-3
  2. EPA: Eicosapentaenoic acid C20:5 n-3
  3. DHA: Docosahexaenoic acid C22:6 n-3

Key Fact: EPA & DHA are primarily marine-derived fatty acids (found in oily fish like salmon, sardines).

Precursor Conversion Pathway

Dietary ALA converts to EPA & DHA in human body!

3. ω-6 (Omega-6) Fatty Acids

• Definition: In ω-6 fatty acids, the first double bond appears at the 6th carbon atom counting from the methyl (CH₃) end of the carbon chain.

• Source Richness: Vegetable oils (Corn oil, Sunflower oil, Soybean oil) are exceptionally rich in ω-6 fatty acids.

Major Members of ω-6 Fatty Acids
Linoleic Acid (LA) Common in plant seed oils
C18:2 n-6
Arachidonic Acid (AA) Precursor to signaling molecules
C20:4 n-6
Docosapentaenoic Acid (DPA) Omega-6 variant of DPA
C22:5 n-6

4. ω-9 (Omega-9) Fatty Acids & Structure

• Definition: First double bond appears at the 9th carbon atom from the methyl (CH₃) end of the carbon chain.
• Chief Member: Oleic Acid (C18:1 n-9).

COMMON BOARD EXAM MISTAKE!

Don't confuse IUPAC numbering with Omega (ω) numbering!
• IUPAC System: Carbon #1 starts at the Carboxyl Carbon (-COOH).
• Omega (ω) System: Carbon #1 starts at the Methyl Carbon (-CH₃) end!
